# How about a little extra?

New Tricks are features that 2dog adds on top of what official Godot provides.
They live in [2dog's Godot fork](https://github.com/outfox/godot), along with most of 2dog's own tweaks fixes, but they are unlikely to make it back into upstream Godot official.

![A white anthro dog in a hacker hoodie pouring syrup on a stack of pancakes, next to their blue robot dog balancing its own stack](../../img/2dog-pancakes.png)     
*(the regular serving is already pretty good, though)*

## A word of caution...

::: warning  
New Tricks may deviate from official Godot. Additionally, some are experimental, and they may  
break compatibility on some platforms.  
:::

- A trick may work on some platforms only, or only in some
[build variants](/build-configurations).
- A project that depends on a trick may not run on official Godot builds.
- Behavior may change between 2dog releases while a trick matures.
